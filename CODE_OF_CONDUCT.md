# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participants must communicate respectfully, discuss technical decisions in good faith, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or publication of another person's private information.

## Privacy and Safety

Do not post cookies, account credentials, private URLs, personal media, private logs, or another person's identifying information in issues, pull requests, discussions, or release notes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when behavior threatens community safety, privacy, or constructive collaboration. Report conduct concerns through the project's maintainer contact once that private channel is published.
